MISSION PROFILE

What IONOSPHERE-AI Monitors

◎

Total Electron Content

TEC represents the amount of free electrons along a signal path through the ionosphere. Changes in TEC can affect satellite navigation and radio communication.

✦

Space Weather

Solar and geomagnetic conditions are incorporated through OMNI measurements to provide additional environmental information for prediction.

⌁

GPS Environment

IONOSPHERE-AI analyses ionospheric conditions that may contribute to changes in satellite navigation signal behaviour.

◇

AI Prediction

A trained Random Forest model uses historical observations and engineered features to estimate TEC values.
